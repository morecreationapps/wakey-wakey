Help with Wakey-Wakey!

Your rota, sleep plans and everyday tasks in one place. Support is provided by MORE CREATION APPS LIMITED.

Tell us which device and app version you use, what you expected to happen and what happened instead. Please do not include passwords, verification codes or unnecessary private rota or health details.

Creating an account or signing in

Create an account with your email and password, confirm the code sent to that address and log in when prompted. Check your junk folder if the code has not arrived. Use the account-recovery option if you have forgotten your password.

If the app asks you to choose between an account copy and a device copy, review and export the offered backups before choosing. Contact support if you are unsure which copy to use.

Shift Transition and Shift Plan

Shift Transition shows preparation for the calendar day before the selected work shift. Shift Plan shows the timings on the workday. Your rota, sleep preferences, travel time and preparation time shape the suggestions.

Tasks remain editable. Fixed appointments retain their entered times; review any conflicts and confirm your employer's rota when plans change.

Notifications

Enable alerts in Settings and allow device or browser notifications. On iPhone or iPad, the website must be added to the Home Screen and opened there before Web Push can be enabled.

Native apps schedule a rolling set of up to 60 alerts within the next 14 days. Reopen the app to refresh it. Web background alerts require a recent app refresh within 30 days and are checked by the server every minute.

Focus, silent mode, battery settings, network access and permissions can delay or block alerts. Use a separate phone alarm for waking up.

Your data and account

Settings offers private backup export and planner reset. Resetting the planner keeps your login. Full account deletion is separate: use Settings → Delete my account in versions offering that option, or email us from the account address to request it. We will verify ownership before removing live account data.

Read the privacy information for storage, notification and deletion details. Keep exported backups private and remove any copies you no longer need.

Sleep planning

Sleep opportunities and task times are planning suggestions. The diary contains your own estimates; the app does not measure sleep or give medical advice. Follow your workplace fatigue procedures and seek appropriate help when sleepiness affects safe work or travel.
