Your planner. Your privacy.

Privacy information for Wakey-Wakey! on iOS, Android, Mac and the website or home-screen web app. Updated 13 October 2026.

MORE CREATION APPS LIMITED operates Wakey-Wakey! and is responsible for the personal information described here. Contact support@morecreationapps.com with privacy questions or requests.

What you provide

  • Your account: email address, password and verification or recovery codes. Supabase Auth manages authentication; your password is not included in your planner records.
  • Your preferences: an optional name and job role, work timezone, travel and preparation times, routines, date and time formats, and sleep and wake preferences.
  • Your rota and tasks: duties, hours, rest days, leave, overtime, appointments, task names, dates, durations, completion states and any location or notes you choose to enter. A sickness or leave entry may reveal health information.
  • Optional sleep diary: your own estimates of bedtime, wake time, sleep duration, awakenings and restfulness. The app does not measure sleep or read HealthKit, Health Connect or wearable data.
  • Support: your email address and the information you choose to send when asking for help.

Location fields contain text you enter. Wakey-Wakey! does not request GPS, contact-book, microphone or camera access. Avoid adding information about other people or sensitive details that are unnecessary for your planner.

Why we use it

We use your information to create and protect your account, verify your email, restore account access, save and synchronise your planner, calculate your personal schedule, provide reminders you enable, handle support and prevent abuse.

We use ordinary account and planning information to provide the service you request. We use necessary technical information to protect and maintain that service. Optional sleep records support only the diary and planning features you choose; they are not used for advertising or decisions about your employment, insurance or fitness for duty.

New sleep-diary entries ask for explicit permission to save your self-reported sleep and restfulness with your account. You can leave the diary unused, request removal of diary entries, reset your planner or delete your account. Sleep plans are editable estimates and are not medical advice.

On your device and in your account

Your planner is saved to your signed-in account in Supabase and cached on your device. Account ownership checks restrict access to the appropriate account. Connections to the account service use HTTPS.

Native apps use SQLite for the local planner and the operating system's secure storage for login sessions. The planner database is not separately encrypted by Wakey-Wakey!. The web app stores its local planner and session in browser storage. Protect your device and avoid leaving an account signed in on a shared device.

This service is not end-to-end encrypted. Authorised service operators and infrastructure providers may access information when needed to operate, secure or support the service.

Notifications

Notifications are optional. Native iOS and Android apps schedule local device notifications; the app does not register a native remote-push token. An open app can also show an in-app alert.

If you enable website or home-screen Web Push, Supabase stores a browser-generated device identifier, the push subscription endpoint and keys, notification preferences, scheduled messages and delivery records. Due activity titles and times are sent through your browser's push service. They may appear on your lock screen; use your device's notification privacy settings if needed.

You can disable alerts in Settings and revoke notification permission in your device or browser settings. Web delivery claims are pruned after 30 days; web devices become ineligible for background delivery after 30 days without an app refresh. That eligibility limit does not itself delete the subscription.

Services that help run the app

  • Supabase: authentication, account planner storage and the website's reminder service. The database project is configured in London, but this is not a promise that every service, log, support operation or subprocessor processes data only in the UK.
  • Resend: verification and recovery emails sent through Supabase. Resend processes the recipient address and email content. Its published policy states that stored email content and logs are processed in the United States, even when an email-sending region is European.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website and these public information pages. Opening them sends normal web-request information to the hosting service.
  • Your browser or device provider: delivers enabled notifications and handles permissions, local storage and any operating-system backup you have configured.
  • Business email providers: handle support correspondence sent to our support address.

These providers have their own security, retention and international-processing arrangements. Their policies include Supabase privacy, Resend privacy and GitHub privacy. Contact us for details of the arrangements relevant to your request.

Wakey-Wakey! has no advertising or analytics SDK and does not sell your planner data, share it with data brokers or send it to an AI API. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites for advertising.

Technical and security records

Account and infrastructure providers may retain security or operational records such as account identifiers, login events, IP addresses, user-agent information, timestamps, delivery outcomes and errors. These help provide and protect the service. They are separate from advertising tracking and from the planner records shown in the app.

Keeping and deleting information

Your active account and planner remain until you remove information, reset the planner or delete the account. Resetting the planner retains your login account. Full account deletion removes the live account and its associated planner records, roles and web-reminder records.

Use Settings → Delete my account in versions offering that control, or email support@morecreationapps.com from your account address to request deletion. We may verify account ownership before acting. Do not send us your password or a verification code.

Provider security logs, previously sent emails and protected backups may remain for their limited operational or legal retention periods; deleting a planner does not instantly erase those separate systems. Resend's published standard email-content and log retention is 30 days, with backup retention of 7 days. Supabase log and backup retention depends on the service plan and configuration.

Support correspondence is retained while needed to resolve the request and maintain necessary business or legal records. If a record must be retained for a specific obligation or dispute, we restrict its use to that purpose. Contact us for the retention details applying to a particular request.

Your controls and rights

You can edit your planner, export a private backup, remove entries, turn off reminders, log out or request account deletion. Privacy laws may also give you rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. These rights depend on the circumstances and applicable law.

For a privacy request, contact support@morecreationapps.com. UK users can also raise concerns with the Information Commissioner's Office; other users can contact their local supervisory authority.

Imports, exports and other devices

Only files you select are imported. Imported planner content is saved with your account. Exported backups can contain rota notes, tasks and sleep records. You choose their destination and are responsible for copies you keep or share outside the app.

Deleting an account cannot remotely erase independently exported files or an offline device's storage. Remove local copies and exports on other devices as needed. An online device must pass account checks to reopen the planner.

Who the app is for

Wakey-Wakey! is designed for adult shift workers and personal planning. It is not a service designed for children, a medical device or an employer monitoring tool. We do not use planner information to assess another person's eligibility, health, employment or fitness for duty.

Changes

We update this page when the service's data practices change. The updated date appears at the top. Material changes that require a new permission will be presented through the app or an appropriate account notice.
